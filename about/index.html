SPACE CASUALS este un studio de arhitectura si design interior cu sediul in Bucuresti, Romania. SPACE CASUALS is an architecture and interior design studio based in Bucharest, Romania.

Suntem un grup dinamic de arhitecti si designeri tineri si motivati, cu experiente de munca anterioare atat in Romania, cat si in strainatate. Team-leaderii grupului sunt acum Alexandru CALIN si Georgiana GOGOLAN. We are a dynamic group of young, motivated architects and designers with previous work experience both in Romania and abroad. The team is currently led by Alexandru CALIN and Georgiana GOGOLAN.

Incepand din 2003, echipa condusa de catre Alexandru CALIN a lucrat sub diferite nume si configuratii, colaborand pentru diverse misiuni atat in proiecte de arhitectura cat si in ceea ce priveste designul de interior. Since 2003, the team led by Alexandru CALIN has worked under various names and configurations, collaborating on both architecture and interior design projects.

Abordarea Space Casuals este una hiper-pragmatica: raspunsul prompt la solicitarile clientului si ale programului intr-un mod creativ si eficient. Space Casuals' approach is hyper-pragmatic: prompt, creative and efficient responses to each client's needs.
