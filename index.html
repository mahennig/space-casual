Un studio
hiper-pragmatic A
hyper-pragmatic studio

SPACE CASUALS este un studio de arhitectura si design interior cu sediul in Bucuresti, Romania. Suntem un grup dinamic de arhitecti si designeri tineri si motivati, cu experiente de munca anterioare atat in Romania, cat si in strainatate. SPACE CASUALS is an architecture and interior design studio based in Bucharest, Romania. We are a dynamic group of young, motivated architects and designers with work experience both in Romania and abroad.

Incepand din 2003, echipa condusa de Alexandru CALIN a lucrat sub diverse forme, colaborand in proiecte de arhitectura si design interior. Abordarea noastra este una hiper-pragmatica: raspuns prompt, creativ si eficient la solicitarile clientului. Since 2003, the team led by Alexandru CALIN has collaborated on architecture and interior design projects. Our approach is hyper-pragmatic: prompt, creative and efficient responses to each client's needs.
